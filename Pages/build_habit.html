Build a Habit

Pick one goal, add tiny steps that make sense for your kid/family, and add a reward.

1. Start with one goal, be specific

Example: “Read 10 minutes,” “Finish algebra homework,” “Practice piano for 30 minutes.”

2. Add tiny steps to keep you accountable

We won’t guess the steps — add the ones that actually work in your home.

    3. Pick your reward

    Add whatever is motivating for this kid — screen time, sticker, high-five, snack.

    Congrats!

    Your habit is ready.
